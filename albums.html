My favourite albums

Ces albums sont mes classiques, ceux auxquels je retourne le plus souvent. Il a été assez difficile de faire un choix et de ne pas mettre tout ce qui me passait pas la tête, mais j'ai réussi à me retrouver avec une petite sélection par genres qui permet de se repérer un peu j'espère :).
Je vous invite à naviguer avec un esprit ouvert entre les différentes catégories même si vous ne vous intéressez pas forcément à un certain genre musical, car au sein des catégories se nichent des petites pépites mélangeant différents genre musicaux et pouvant vous ouvrir à un autre monde.
.

Jazz

Anne Paceo - Circles

Crazy saxophones and voices, with electronic and hip hop influences and great improvs
Favourite track: Toundra

Sissoko, Segal, Parisien, Peirani - Les Egarés

Great musicians (kora, cello, saxophone and accordion) united to produce this pure pépite.
Favourite track: Esperanza

Leïla Martial – Jubilä 432

Genius woman singing and chatting with birds.
Favourite tracks: La rencontre, La bergère

Badbadnotgood – III

Not only jazz but also fusion rock hip hop
Favourite tracks: Triangle, Confessions ft. Leland Whitty

Badbadnotgood – IV

Jazz fusion rock hip hop
Favourite tracks: Time Moves Slow, Chompy’s Paradise, In Your Eyes

Neo soul

The RH Factor – Hard Groove

The groove is extremely hard. For sexy trumpet lovers.
Favourite track: I'll stay

Childish Gambino – Awaken, My Love

Soul/funk/R&B/psychedelic.
Favourite tracks: Zombies, California
Most famous track: Redbone

Yussef Kamaal – Black focus

Yussed Dayes and Kamaal Williams in duo. This music makes me feel high and lose track of time. Jazz funk/broken beat.
Favourite tracks: Remembrance

Polylogue from Sila – Wordz Ain’t Good

Electric soul, a nice mix of genres with hidden nods to French politics.
Favourite tracks: Lobster Lovers

Trip hop/downtempo

Erykah Badu – Mama’s gun

Mainly (beautiful) songs about love.
Favourite tracks: Didn’t Cha Know, …& On, Orange Moon, In Love With You

Massive Attack – Blue lines

I love every album of Massive Attack but if I had to choose one, now it would be this one.
Favourite tracks: One Love

Air – Moon Safari

I also adore every album of Air; this one is the first I discovered.
Favourite track: Sexy Boy, All I Need

The Troublemakers – Doubts & Convictions

Electronic groovy French group. The track Get Misunderstood is used in radio jingles (France Musique and France Culture).
Favourite tracks: Get Misunderstood, Fatigue universelle

Gorillaz - Gorillaz

My favourite album from them is their first one!
Favourite tracks: Double bass, Starshine

Stoner rock/progressive rock/doom metal/groove metal

Step in fluid – One step beyond

I discovered this group on a niche radio emission. The groove is outstandingly present. Crazy free jazz saxophone improvs in the middle of progressive metal tracks.
Favourite tracks: Smooth, As we dance

Mars red sky – Mars red sky

Discovered in a rock festival. It helps me to calm down when I’m angry, and it’s my favourite album to get motivated to clean. Stoner/psychedelic rock.
Favourite track: Falls

Conjurer – Páthos

EP sludge metal/doom metal/post metal
Favourite tracks: In Your Wake, Those Years Condemned

1782 – 1782

Doom doom metal.
Favourite track: She Was A Witch

Alternative rock

The White Stripes – Elephant

A classic that I know since I’m a child. My parents used to go to the same bar all the time when I was little, and we kids got to choose the music. We always played that, the Black Eyed Peas and LMFAO. I also love Seven Nation Army’s cover by Ben l’Oncle Soul.
Favourite tracks: Seven Nation Army (obviously), In the cold cold night

Pixies – Wave of Mutilation

I love to listen to this album on the road.
Favourite tracks: Caribou, Vamos

Blur – 13

Trippy album that makes you lose track of time.
Favourite tracks: Tender, Battle

Baroque music related

Raphaël Imbert – Bach Coltrane

Crazy saxophonist mixes Bach’s works with jazz improvisations. He Nevuh Said a Mumbalin tells a part of the Bible in which Mary loses her son Jesus. The verse describing her grief is replaced by a very expressive saxophone passage.
Favourite track: He Nevuh Said a Mumbalin' Word II

Wendy Carlos - Switched on Bach

Electronic Bach from 1968!! Wendy Carlos is experimenting Bach's music on a Moog synthetizer. She is very protective with her music, so you actually have to buy it to listen to it (or contact me).
Favourite track: Air on a G String

Béla Fleck – Perpetual motion

Imrovisations based on classical composers such as Bach or… but on banjo, with bluegrass accents from time to time.
Favourite tracks: Rhapsodie in blue, Keyboard Sonata in D minor

Max Richter – Vivaldi: The Four Seasons

The four seasons revisited in an oniric way by Max Richter, with an extra newly composed season: Shadows. Very relaxing and to listen to with heavy bass.
Favourite track: Summer 1

Jozef van Wissem – New Lute Music for Film

Really dark music for vampires from another time. You can tell from the way the lute is tuned that this music is not intended for humans from the 21th century.
Favourite track: The Purified Eye of the Soul is Placed in the Circle of the Eternal Sun

Electro

Vitalic – OK Cowboy

Electro/techno album from 2005. Vitalic is part of the duo Kompromat with Rebeka Warrior.
Favourite track: Polkamatic

Birdy Nam Nam – Birdy Nam Nam Live

Live mixing, using hip-hop, groove and jazz samples.
Favourite tracks: Poppy, Violons (Part 1)

Fred Pallem & Le Sacre du Tympan - François de Roubaix

I did'nt know where to put this album. It's an hommage to François de Roubaix, so it's inspired by film music and french touch but in an electronic way with lots of synthetizers but also "real" instruments. Philippe Katerine apperas in this album.
Favourite track: L'Atelier

Pop/folk

Jack Johnson & Friends – Best of Kokua Festival

Best of Kokua Festival with many artists such as Ziggy Marley, Taj Mahal, G Love and Paula Fuga. Cool kid surf music.
Favourite track: High Tide or Low Tide ft. Ben Harper

Paula Fuga – Lilikoi

Reggae/folk music from Hawaii. Lilikoiis a type of passionfruit (Passiflora edulis).
Favourite track: Lilikoi

Dino Brandao, Sophie Hunger, Faber – Ich liebe Dich

An ode to love, sometimes a bit melancholic. They sing in swiss german.
Favourite tracks: Putsch, Dr Hunger Wird Schlimmer, Derfi di hebe

Renée Flemming, Béla Fleck – The fiddle and the drum

American/bluegrass/Appalachian music.
Favourite tracks: In the pines ft. Dolly Parton, Pretty bird

Jacob Collier – Djesse Vol. 2

Delicious to my ears. R&B/jazz fusion/folk/experimental pop.
Favourite tracks: Feel ft. Lianne La Havas

Hip hop/Rap

Traditional music

Carré Manchot – Krenn ha krak

Traditional music and dances from Brittany. I had the chance to see them live once and it was FIRE.
Favourite tracks: Hanter dro, Andro

Matt Molloy – Back to the island

Traditional irish tunes on flute.
Favourite track: The Girl I'll Ne'er Forget
